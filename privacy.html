Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04

In short: Viksen does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything it does with your microphone, motion and health data happens on your own devices.

Microphone

With your permission, Viksen listens to your surroundings and transforms the sound live. Audio is processed on your device in real time. Short passages may be held in temporary memory (for example for the "Room memory" effect); they are never written to storage and are erased when the microphone is turned off. Viksen has no recording feature: nothing you hear or that the microphone picks up is ever recorded, saved or uploaded.

Motion, activity and head tracking

With your permission, Viksen uses your device's motion sensors, activity and step cadence (Motion & Fitness) and the head motion of compatible headphones, so the sound can respond to how you move. This data is used live on your device and is not stored or transmitted.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you enable it, Viksen reads heart rate, resting heart rate, workouts and your date of birth (to estimate your maximum heart rate) from Apple Health, and the Viksen Apple Watch app sends live heart rate from a workout to your iPhone. This data is used only to adapt the sound to your exercise intensity. It never leaves your devices, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Weather and location (optional)

If you turn on weather-aware atmospheres, Viksen asks for your approximate location and sends it to Apple's WeatherKit service to obtain the current weather. Viksen does not store your location. Apple's handling of this request is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Weather is off unless you turn it on.

Your music and files

Songs and sounds you import are copied into Viksen's own storage on your device. Your original files are never modified or uploaded.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Viksen receives only Apple's confirmation of which editions you own; it never sees your payment details.

Diagnostics

Viksen contains no analytics or tracking code. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports.

Children

Viksen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
