Tips

Live microphone effects: use wired headphones for the lowest delay. Bluetooth headphones add noticeable latency, and using the speaker with the microphone on can cause feedback.

Head tracking: needs compatible AirPods or Beats. Use "Recenter" to set your new front.

Restore purchases: Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases.

Safety: immersive audio can hide important sounds. Keep volume moderate and stay aware of your surroundings.
